Our Approach

Unhurried consultations. Same-day diagnostics.

Every consultation happens in a private suite, with time set aside to listen. If your doctor needs a blood test, X-ray or ECG, it is done on site, and most reports reach your doctor the same day.

  • Fixed time slots, so you are seen close to your booked time
  • Prescriptions filled at the in-house pharmacy
  • Follow-up reminders on your phone
